# Claude Design brief: Crystal Kizor landing page

Design a single-page personal-brand landing page for Crystal Kizor, an architect based in Enugu State, Nigeria. Her focus is building for hot climates (passive cooling, local materials). Connecting idea: "Building better for people and place."

## Motion format (inspired by a scroll-snap template, adapted)

1. **Panel wipe (slide-over).** The first four sections (Hero, Idea, Principles, Proof) are full-screen panels. As you scroll, each new panel slides up and covers the previous one, which stays pinned. This uses normal native scrolling: no scroll-jacking, no one-flick-per-section snapping.
2. **Word rail.** In the hero, under the headline, one role word sits in a one-line window and ticks upward odometer-style through: Architect, Designer, Entrepreneur, Speaker, Researcher, Creator. Use a small version in the header beside the wordmark.
3. **Panel counter and progress.** A thin hairline progress bar and a counter in the form "01 ——— 04" show which pinned panel you are on.
4. **Vertical SCROLL label.** Small uppercase "SCROLL" text, rotated 90°, at the bottom left of the hero.
5. After the four panels, the page scrolls normally with soft fade-up reveals on headlines and images.
6. Phones and reduced motion: everything scrolls normally with no pinning and no ticking rail (show the roles as a plain line).

Do not use colour-inverting blend modes. Colours must stay on-palette.

## Visual direction

Refined architectural editorial. Oversized expressive serif headlines, clean sans body text, generous white space, thin 1px dividers, photography-led. Each section gets its own composition. No rows of identical cards, no generic SaaS look.

- **Colours:** canvas #F3EEE5 (main surface), surface #E8E0D4, surface-muted #D5C8B7, sand #B5A18A, taupe #C2AD96, clay #A75F3B (key actions and small accents only), ink #28251F (text), stone-dark #302B25 (dark sections and the full-screen menu), muted text #71685E (only on canvas or surface), border #C9BDAE, warm white #FAF7F1.
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond (display), DM Sans (body). Headline tracking -0.045em. Labels 12px uppercase, tracking 0.12em.
- **Shape:** buttons 8px radius, images 12px, feature panels 20px. Shadows rare and soft.
- **Section labels:** "04 / STUDIO COKA" style, next to a thin vertical hairline. Text links are uppercase with a line and arrow: "VIEW ALL PROJECTS ——>".
- **Header:** "CRYSTAL KIZOR." text wordmark on the left, a "MENU" label with two short lines on the right, and a "Work with her" button. The menu opens full-screen on stone-dark with numbered oversized serif links.

## Content rules

- Use ONLY the copy below. Do not invent numbers, quotes, clients, awards, products or people.
- No stock or AI images of people or buildings. Use clearly labelled grey placeholders such as "[Photo: Crystal portrait]".
- Refer to Crystal in the third person.

## Sections, in order

1. **Hero** (panel). Headline (uppercase, multi-line): "I show how we can build better homes, buildings and cities for hot climates." Roles rail: Architect, designer, entrepreneur, speaker, researcher and creator. Asymmetric: text on one side, image placeholder (project photo showing shade, openings and light) on the other. Buttons: "See her work", "Get in touch".
2. **The Idea** (panel). "Architecture changes lives, improves living standards, and can be the difference between survival and thriving." Portrait placeholder. Thin line-art diagram: Crystal at the centre, branching to Studio COKA, The Effective Architect, The ROI of Intentional Design, AKO Alliance, Alive and Free.
3. **Principles** (panel). Six passive-cooling principles with thin line-art icons: Thick walls, Large openings, High-level openings, Deep overhangs, Indoor-outdoor living, Trees as part of the design.
4. **Proof** (panel, dark over a project photo placeholder). Four large metrics with fine dividers: 100,000+ sqm designed and coordinated / About 95% less diesel at an off-grid eye hospital / About ₦8M saved a year / 10,000+ community in The Effective Architect.
5. **Studio COKA** (largest section). Tagline (italic serif): "Built for this climate. Designed for People." Text: A climate-responsive design and build studio, founded in 2024, helping home and business owners design and build intentional spaces. Services: Architecture | Interior Design | Project Management. Project slider, one at a time, with a counter: Nature Home (Enugu State, Nigeria), Eye hospital (Nsukka, Nigeria), House of the Future (UAE, 2025 prototype), Kasa Kofo Community Centre (Casamance, Senegal, proposal). One dominant image with asymmetric smaller previews. Button: "Start a project".
6. **Learn.** Two different compositions, not matching cards. The Effective Architect: a global platform training architects and designers to think clearly, design intentionally and execute at a high level. Community of 10,000+. Button "Join the waitlist". The ROI of Intentional Design: her weekly newsletter on what good design returns in value, comfort, health and quality of life. Button "Subscribe on LinkedIn".
7. **Impact.** Warm, human photo strip with rounded 20px images. AKO Alliance: an NGO working to return Nigeria's out-of-school children to school, support families with business capital and back new ideas. Alive and Free: a movement for young people exploring identity, purpose and life in Christ, through honest conversations, gatherings and worship. Equal weight. "Learn more" on each.
8. **Speak & Write.** Heading "Speaking. Writing. Sharing." Large video placeholder for her TEDx talk on integrating Indigenous Architecture into modern living (2025), with a small thumbnail row. Writing: posts and series on passive cooling, building local and intentional design. Button "Get in touch".
9. **Where to go next.** Heading "What are you here for?" Five editorial rows (not cards): I want to build a home or space / I want to learn how to design like this / I want to support children's education / I want to book Crystal to speak / I want to read her ideas.
10. **About.** Bio placeholder "[Bio from Crystal]". Second portrait placeholder. Based in Enugu State, Nigeria. Graduate Member, Nigerian Institute of Architects. Member, Green Building Council Nigeria.
11. **Contact and footer.** Warm taupe, calm and spacious (not a heavy dark footer). Contact invitation, brand names linking out, compact navigation, "© 2026 Crystal Kizor. All rights reserved."
